## What is wrong now

The scene reads "a playground is a place", which is the right idea, but the children are tiny stick figures from the old
props kit, the slide and swing are thin poles hard to make out from the seat, and the swing copies 女の子's scene (a girl
on a swing). Rebuild it with the Quaternius children, a sturdier slide and a **sandbox instead of the swing**.

## What the scene must show (a loop of about 8 s)

- **While the strokes draw:** an empty patch of grass beside the kanji: a thin green block (about 1.4u wide, 0.7u
  deep, 0.03u thick), its top on the ground line, tipped about 0.3 rad toward the viewer so the learner sees its top.
- **Then the playground pops up out of the grass:** a slide (red frame, yellow chute, a few steps up its back) and a
  sandbox (a low wooden frame full of yellow sand), as the slide and swing pop up now.
  - The slide: its steps about 0.35u right of the kanji, its platform about 0.3u high, the chute running down to the
    right to about 0.95u; behind the middle of the grass (a little away from the viewer).
  - The sandbox: about 0.3u wide, between 1.0u and 1.35u right of the kanji, nearer the viewer.
- **The girl (gal3, a child):** walks in to the foot of the steps, walks up them (rising with each step, feet on the
  steps), sits down on the platform, slides down the chute sitting (her hips on the chute's surface all the way), lands
  at the bottom, jumps up cheering (Victory), walks round to the steps and starts again.
- **The boy (guy2, a child):** sits on the sandbox's rim (hips on the rim), scoops sand with a small red spade held
  against his palm (the PickUp clip, looped, or `handTo`), puffs of sand fly; a little sand castle grows in front of him
  in three steps and stays.
- Nothing covers the kanji or the meaning label under it. Everything within 1.4u right of the kanji.
